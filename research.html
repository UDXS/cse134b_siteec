Research Interests

EDA

Works

  • A. B. Kahng, S. Kang, S. Kundu, Y. Liu, D. Markarian, S. Park and Z. Wang, "Invited: Post-Placement Buffering and Sizing Contest"

    The ISPD 2026 Contest challenges participants to develop post-detailed placement buffering and sizing tools that optimize timing and fix electrical rule check (ERC) violations under real-world constraints. Worked on benchmarking, isolated and reproducible evaluation, contestant scoring. Presented the results at ISPD 2026 in Bonn, Germany.

  • Unreleased paper on Secure Microarchitecture

    Built on RISC-V technology and a customized LLVM compiler. Drove the custom ISA extension implementation in LLVM. Details to be added on release.
