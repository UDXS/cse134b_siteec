The Cameras

Sigma dp2 Quattro

Exceptional Image. Incredible size.

The Foveon sensor is unique, punchy, filmic, but delicate. dp2 Quattro represents Sigma's first step into building cameras that feel great, not just look great. With a striking ultracompact body, a brighter display, faster (though still slow) processing, and improved battery, this camera represents the best of Foveon X3.
